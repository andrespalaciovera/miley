## Notes for the hifi build
- This product intentionally exercises the **Benefit Chips** variant of Product Profile, the **Facts Table** variant of Facts Panel (true rows/columns with %DV — structurally different from coffee's icon-stat cards), and the **Instruction List** variant of Preparation/Usage (a dosage list, not numbered brewing steps).
- The FDA disclaimer line is included deliberately — real supplement PDPs almost universally carry this exact language near dosage instructions, and it reinforces the claims-vs-facts distinction: Benefit Chips are marketing language (chips), while the Facts Table is regulated, verifiable data (a table) — the visual treatment should make that difference legible, not just decorative.
